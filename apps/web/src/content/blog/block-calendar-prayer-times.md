If you work in a corporate environment with back-to-back meetings, you know the struggle: Dhuhr comes and goes while you're in a meeting, and you didn't even realize it.

## Why calendar blocking matters

Calendar blocking is the simplest way to protect your prayer time. When your calendar shows you as "busy" during prayer times, colleagues won't schedule meetings over them. In Microsoft Teams and Outlook, your status automatically changes to "Busy" — no explanation needed.

## How Waqtu does it automatically

1. **Grant calendar access** — Waqtu asks for permission to create events
2. **Choose your calendar** — Select Exchange, iCloud, or your default
3. **Enable calendar protection** — Turn it on in Settings → General
4. **Done** — Waqtu creates daily prayer blocks, refreshed at midnight

## What your colleagues see

In Outlook, your calendar shows "Prayer – Dhuhr" as busy blocks. Teams automatically reflects your Outlook status. Colleagues simply see you as unavailable.

## Customizing your blocks

- **Duration** — Set each block to 10–20 minutes
- **Reminders** — 5-min calendar alert before each prayer
- **Calendar choice** — Pick Exchange for Teams sync

## Pro tips

- Use Exchange/Outlook so Teams reflects your status
- 15-minute blocks give enough time for wudu and prayer
- Events refresh daily at midnight for accurate times
- Everything happens locally on your Mac — no internet needed
