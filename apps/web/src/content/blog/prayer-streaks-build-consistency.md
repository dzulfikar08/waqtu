Consistency is the hardest part of salah. Anyone can pray five times on a good day. The challenge is doing it every day — through meetings, travel, and fatigue.

Waqtu's prayer streak tracking turns consistency into a visible, motivating habit. Here's how it works and why it matters.

## Why streaks work

Streak tracking works because it changes your relationship with consistency. Missing a prayer isn't just a spiritual loss — it breaks a visible chain. That visual cue creates accountability that a mental note never can.

Research on habit formation shows that tracking progress is one of the most effective ways to build lasting habits. The same principle that makes Duolingo streaks and fitness rings effective applies to salah.

## How Waqtu tracks your prayers

- **Automatic logging** — When you click "I am going to pray" on the overlay, Waqtu marks that prayer as complete
- **Missed prayer detection** — If you dismiss the overlay but keep working at your Mac, Waqtu notices and gently reminds you
- **Manual control** — Your prayer log is always accurate because it's tied to your actual actions

## Your streak report

In the Waqtu menu bar, expand the Streak section to see:

- **Current streak** — How many consecutive days you've completed all 5 prayers
- **Best streak** — Your all-time record
- **Today's status** — Which prayers are done, pending, or missed
- **Weekly grid** — A 7-day heatmap showing your consistency patterns
- **Weekly percentage** — e.g. "15/25 (60%)"

## The accountability feature

Waqtu includes a gentle accountability mechanism. After you tap "I am going to pray," Waqtu watches for 5 minutes. If you stay at your Mac — moving the mouse, typing — it sends a simple reminder:

## Privacy note

Your prayer log is stored locally on your Mac in UserDefaults. It never leaves your device. No account required. No cloud sync (unless you want iCloud in a future update).
