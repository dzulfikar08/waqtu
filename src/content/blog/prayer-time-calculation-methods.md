One of the most common frustrations with prayer apps is that times don't match the local mosque. This is usually because the app uses a different calculation method. Here's what each method means and how to choose the right one.

## Why there are different methods

Prayer times are based on the position of the sun. While Dhuhr, Asr, and Maghrib are relatively straightforward, Fajr and Isha depend on twilight angles — and different scholarly opinions use different angles. Additionally, high-latitude locations (above 48°N) need special rules because twilight can persist all night in summer.

## Common calculation methods

### Muslim World League (MWL)

**Fajr angle:** 18° | **Isha angle:** 17°  
Used by: Europe, Far East, parts of Americas. This is the default in most apps and a good starting point if you're unsure.

### ISNA (Islamic Society of North America)

**Fajr angle:** 15° | **Isha angle:** 15°  
Used by: Most North American mosques. If you're in the US or Canada, this is likely what your local mosque uses. ISNA uses equal angles for Fajr and Isha.

### Egyptian General Authority of Survey

**Fajr angle:** 19.5° | **Isha angle:** 17.5°  
Used by: Egypt, parts of Africa, and some Middle Eastern countries. A bit earlier Fajr than MWL.

### Umm al-Qura (Makkah)

**Fajr angle:** 18.5° | **Isha:** 90 minutes after Maghrib  
Used by: Saudi Arabia and the Arabian Peninsula. Note: Isha is fixed at 90 minutes after Maghrib, not based on an angle.

### University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi

**Fajr angle:** 18° | **Isha angle:** 18°  
Used by: Pakistan, Bangladesh, India, Afghanistan. Equal Fajr/Isha angles.

### Moonsighting Committee

**Fajr angle:** 15° | **Isha angle:** 15°  
Used by: Parts of North America and UK. Based on actual moonsighting methodology.

## Asr calculation: Shafi vs Hanafi

In addition to the method, there's the Asr timing difference:

- **Shafi/Maliki/Hanbali (earlier):** Asr begins when an object's shadow equals its length.
- **Hanafi (later):** Asr begins when an object's shadow equals twice its length. ~45–60 minutes later.
