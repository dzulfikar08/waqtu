You check your menu bar dozens of times a day — for the time, your WiFi, your battery, your Slack notifications. Now add prayer times to that list.

## The problem with phone-based prayer apps

Most Muslims use their phone to check prayer times. But at work, pulling out your phone every hour is (a) distracting, (b) looks unprofessional, and (c) means you might forget to check entirely.

A phone app requires you to *remember* to open it. A menu bar app shows you the time *without you remembering*.

## Why the menu bar works

- **Always visible** — A quick glance tells you the next prayer and countdown. No apps to open.
- **Discreet** — It's just an icon and time. Colleagues won't know what you're checking.
- **Contextual** — You're already on your Mac for work. Prayer times live where you work.
- **No friction** — Zero clicks to see prayer times. Information radiators work.
